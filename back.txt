I am a second year PhD student of Computer Sciences at University of Wisconsin Madison. I have an undergraduate background in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT Kharagpur, India. My research interests are broadly in Machine Learning and Computer Vision. I am interested in both theory and application of machine learning to solve real life problems. I am currently working on a project involving information theoretic measures for improving generalization and applicability to medical imaging datasets. I am advised by Prof. Vikas Singh. In the past I have worked on projects involving machine teaching which used techniques from control theory specially model predictive control to explore minial number of data points required for achieving convergence in a teaching task. My Bachelor's thesis involved visual saliency prediction for autonomous driving. I have also worked on the topic of visual saliency prediction for mobile user interfaces during my research internship at Adobe.
