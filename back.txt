I am a second year PhD student of Computer Sciences at University of Wisconsin Madison. I have a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT Kharagpur, India. My research interests are broadly in Machine Learning and Computer Vision. I am interested in both theory and application of machine learning to solve real life problems. I am currently working on a project involving information theoretic measures for improving generalization and applicability to medical imaging datasets, specifically ones with small dataset size. I am advised by Prof. Vikas Singh. Previously I worked on a problem revolving around sequential or iterative machine teaching using techniques from control theory, namely model predictive control to explore minimum number of data points required for achieving convergence in a sequential machine teaching scenario which turns out to be lower than the bounds obtained using greedy approaches in different settings. My Bachelor's thesis involved visual saliency prediction for autonomous driving. I have also worked on the topic of visual saliency prediction for mobile user interfaces during my research internship at Adobe.
